# Ravensmoor Recurring Characters

Sixteen important characters form the starting cast for an episodic campaign
across the villages, woods and dungeons of the [world setting](setting.md).
They have lives and relationships before meeting the player. Their loyalties
can change as adventures affect their homes, ambitions and one another.

Names and biographies are working proposals. Tomas, Ivo, Dain and Sera develop
the existing town guard, alchemist, Greybridge smith and hooded stranger roles;
their identities are not yet established in the game. Existing dialogue,
portraits and quest events remain unchanged by this document. Future writing
should reconcile these proposals with those events rather than silently
replacing them.

The cast includes friends, potential companions, rivals, dangerous opponents
and six possible romantic interests. All romance candidates are adults.
Relationship turns below are possibilities for later episodes, not a fixed
sequence of betrayals, reconciliations or romances.

## The characters

### 1 Tomas Venn

**Human man, 34. Ravensmoor guard.** Reserved, observant and dryly funny once
he relaxes. He remembers who needs help carrying water and repairs the same
old pair of boots long after he could replace them. He wants people to feel
safe, but tends to confuse protecting someone with deciding for them.

Tomas trusts Marta, buys supplies from Ivo and regularly argues with Anja
about unsafe roads. Veska once pulled him out of a collapsed passage; he still
feels indebted to her. He tolerates Sera because her knowledge has proved
useful, without trusting her explanations.

**Relationship potential:** A steady friendship or a slowly developing romance.
He may surrender information or obstruct the player to protect villagers,
especially if Veska calls in her debt. Becoming a better ally means learning
to share difficult decisions. A broken confidence needs an admission and
changed behaviour before trust can return.

### 2 Marta Wren

**Human woman, 46. Keeper of a small inn and communal kitchen in Ravensmoor.**
Warm, practical and capable of a devastatingly accurate imitation of a pompous
customer. She notices an untouched meal before she notices an expensive sword.
Her hospitality is sincere; so is her tendency to arrange other people's lives.

Pella shares her household and considers herself a partner in running it.
Marta gives Anja a regular bed, has known Tomas since his first watch, and
remembers Ivo before he became the village's dependable expert. She will offer
even Sera a meal, but refuses to let her question guests at the table.

**Relationship potential:** A lasting friend whose affection survives some
disagreements. She can shelter someone the player considers an enemy, or
refuse to conceal conduct that threatens her neighbours. Her support should
include ordinary meals, teasing and a place to talk, not only crisis scenes.

### 3 Ivo Fen

**Human man, 57. The alchemist behind the Ravensmoor stall.** Enthusiastic,
absent-minded about appointments and meticulous about ingredients. He likes
teaching and takes unreasonable pleasure in growing excellent beans. He wants
useful magic to reach ordinary people, but dislikes admitting uncertainty.

Oren learned practical ward work from him. He exchanges plant knowledge with
Elian and trades scavenged glassware with Kett. Sera is an old collaborator;
their friendship cooled after a disagreement about keeping dangerous findings
secret. Pella remembers several experiments he now tells as safer stories.

**Relationship potential:** A generous mentor who can disappoint the player
without becoming an enemy. He might dismiss a warning to defend his reputation,
then struggle to acknowledge the consequences. He can also take a genuine
professional risk on the player's behalf. Repair comes through honest work,
not a revelation that he knew the answer all along.

### 4 Dain Rook

**Human man, 40. Greybridge smith and Anja's older brother.** Patient with
damaged tools, impatient with people who conceal how they broke them. He enjoys
music and quietly makes tiny metal animals from scraps. He wants a secure
workshop and a life in which he is useful without always being needed.

He helped raise Anja and still slips from concern into treating her as a child.
Runa is his former partner; they retain affection and practical trust, although
living together did not suit them. Adrien supplies some of his materials and
pressures him over payment. Dain repairs Nessa's equipment despite their
disagreements over access to woodland resources.

**Relationship potential:** A grounded friend with interests outside combat.
He might refuse an otherwise profitable commission, resent being taken for
granted, or support the player against a patron he cannot easily afford to lose.
He and Anja must be allowed to resolve parts of their relationship themselves.

### 5 Sera Vale

**Human woman, 43. Proposed identity of the hooded stranger.** A knowledgeable
traveller concerned with old wards and the Raven Seal. Controlled in public,
unexpectedly playful over a board game, and habitually economical with the
truth. She wants to prevent harm, but believes she is unusually qualified to
decide which harms are acceptable.

She worked with Ivo, has paid Anja to carry sealed messages, and knows Elian
from earlier journeys. Tomas questions her methods. Adrien sometimes provides
funding, a relationship neither advertises. Her existing help with the
Undercrypt remains real: later complexity should not erase the player's first
successful adventure.

**Relationship potential:** A valuable ally who can become a friend, betray
confidence, or become an adversary. She might divert an entrusted object or
withhold a warning to protect another place. This should follow visible habits
and competing commitments. If she betrays the player, the player can refuse
reconciliation; understanding her reasons does not oblige forgiveness.

### 6 Anja Rook

**Human woman, 29. Courier between the six villages.** Curious, quick to laugh
and good at remembering what people actually asked for. She collects short
local sayings and makes terrible drawings of otherwise accurate routes. She
wants to build a reliable courier service and be recognized for her judgement.

Dain is her protective older brother. Marta hosts her between journeys, and
Tomas is an old friend whose warnings she sometimes hears as instructions.
She travels with Runa when the roads are poor. She carries Sera's letters but
does not know everything they contain.

**Relationship potential:** A recurring travelling friend or romantic interest.
Her routes make reunions natural without making her permanently available.
She will resist being used to spy on correspondents, even for an ally. A
friendship can strain over secrecy without revealing her to be a hidden agent;
her ambition and professional boundaries matter in their own right.

### 7 Elian Reed

**Elf man, 104, an adult. Guide and grower of medicinal plants in the Elderwood.**
Sociable, attentive and fond of elaborate jokes that sometimes outlast their
audience. He understands local customs well but is not an authority on every
elf or spirit. He wants exchange with the villages to remain possible without
turning every path and plant into something for sale.

He trades knowledge with Ivo, works with Anja and has travelled with Sera.
Nessa is a longstanding friend and former trail partner; they disagree about
how much access outsiders should have. He can speak with Vey, who does not
necessarily accept his interpretation of an agreement.

**Relationship potential:** An approachable friend or romantic interest who
initially makes cultural differences seem easy. He avoids some disputes to
remain liked, leaving others to bear their consequences. Later episodes can
require him to take an unpopular position, including against the player,
without making either affection or elven loyalty disappear.

### 8 Nessa Thorn

**Elf woman, 87, an adult. Warden of paths and inhabited woodland boundaries.**
Blunt, disciplined and unexpectedly competitive at games. She is generous with
time once she has promised it, but slow to reconsider an initial judgement.
She wants the woods' inhabitants to have a say in who uses their homes.

She has repeatedly confronted Veska's crews. Dain maintains her equipment,
while Elian challenges her restrictions and remains someone she trusts in an
emergency. She helped Kett's people find temporary shelter after a tunnel
collapse. Adrien would like her boundaries to be more negotiable.

**Relationship potential:** An early adversary who can become a reliable ally,
friend or romantic interest. Trust grows through respecting agreements and
seeing each other act under pressure. Romance does not make her abandon the
people she protects. If her restrictions have hurt innocent travellers, she
must also be able to acknowledge and repair that harm.

### 9 Runa Pike

**Human woman, 36. Reedbank boat captain and occasional smuggler.** Cheerful
under pressure, attentive to her crew and impatient with solemn speeches.
She maintains a small floating garden and dislikes eating alone. She wants
to own her boat outright and choose her work without endangering her crew.

Dain is her former partner and trusted repairer. Anja is a frequent passenger
and friend. Adrien holds a substantial debt against her business. Vey has
previously overturned one of her boats; a crew member died, and neither side
accepts the other's account of what led to it.

**Relationship potential:** A lively ally, close friend or romantic interest
whose evasiveness can become costly. Under financial pressure she might sell
route information or abandon an agreed delivery. She can instead choose to
risk the boat and ask openly for help. A betrayal should have consequences
for people beyond the player, including a crew who can lose trust in her.

### 10 Oren Ash

**Human nonbinary person, 31. Travelling ward mender based in Brookstead.**
Inventive, earnest and delighted by a clever repair. They remember broken
devices better than appointments and are learning to apologize without giving
a lecture. They want useful magical knowledge to be available beyond those
who can pay for it.

Hester is their mother and finds their free work both admirable and exhausting.
Ivo taught them and still argues with their experimental methods. Anja carries
their repair notes, and Kett exchanges practical designs with them. Adrien
resents the effect of freely shared charms on his business.

**Relationship potential:** A friend or romantic interest with a substantial
life between visits. Their principles can conflict with the player's preferred
solution: publishing a useful technique may also help dangerous people. They
can change their mind about safeguards without abandoning their generosity,
and they can ask the player to accept a reasonable disagreement.

### 11 Hester Bell

**Human woman, 62. Brookstead miller and village negotiator.** Capable,
sharp-tongued and proud of keeping promises through bad seasons. She repairs
children's toys while listening to customers. She wants the mill to keep
people fed, but often assumes that years of responsibility settle an argument.

Oren is her adult child, with whom she is learning to negotiate rather than
give orders. She buys repairs from Dain, negotiates grain prices with Adrien,
and disputes water use with Vey. Marta is a friend who can challenge her
without being dismissed as inexperienced.

**Relationship potential:** A civic opponent who may become a respected friend.
She can resist a plan for sensible reasons, even while being wrong about parts
of it. Trust might grow through solving an ordinary supply problem together.
She should not need a dark secret to be interesting or difficult.

### 12 Veska Bracken

**Human woman, 47. Leader of a salvage company based near Bracken Hollow.**
Resourceful, persuasive and brave enough to share physical danger with her
crews. She remembers workers' names and pays promptly, yet treats their right
to refuse dangerous work as an obstacle. She wants control of profitable
dungeon routes and believes success entitles her to set the terms.

She once rescued Tomas. Adrien finances expeditions, Nessa obstructs her
woodland operations, and Kett disputes her claim to passages already inhabited
by kobolds. Some workers genuinely like her; others remain because leaving
would cost them their livelihood.

**Relationship potential:** A recurring enemy who can cooperate during a
collapse or rescue without becoming a friend. She can offer real benefits
and still choose intimidation or violence when challenged. Stopping her may
require separating her workers from her authority. Any eventual change must
include relinquishing power and addressing harm; a sympathetic past alone
does not redeem her.

### 13 Adrien Kest

**Human man, 45. Greybridge merchant and creditor.** Courteous, funny and
excellent at making an invitation feel personal. He enjoys cooking for a
crowded table and can be generous when nobody is watching. He wants reliable
trade and enough influence that no disruption can leave him dependent on
somebody else's goodwill.

He supplies Dain, lends to Runa, bargains with Hester and funds Veska despite
warnings about her methods. Sera has accepted his help. He finds Oren's free
distribution of useful knowledge both impractical and threatening.

**Relationship potential:** A useful patron who can become a coercive adversary
or a limited, accountable partner. He offers genuinely helpful bargains before
asking for concessions. A player can earn his respect without accepting his
control. His generosity and relationships should remain real even if he chooses
to become an enemy; they do not cancel responsibility for those choices.

### 14 Kett

**Adult kobold, they/them. Repairer and negotiator moving between the Broken
Kilns and Old Quarry Warrens.** Patient, suspicious of grand speeches and
affectionate towards people who return borrowed tools. They name particularly
good tools after previous owners. They want their community to have safe homes
and agreements that neighbours actually honour.

Oren exchanges designs with them, Ivo trades for recovered glassware, and Nessa
has previously helped their displaced neighbours. Veska claims salvage rights
over places they still inhabit. Kett knows Anja as someone who delivers a
message even when its recipient is unpopular.

**Relationship potential:** An apparent dungeon adversary who can become an
important ally and friend. Cooperation depends on how the player treats the
community, not only Kett. They may refuse access, conceal a route or take back
supplies when an agreement fails. Friendship never makes them a spokesperson
for every kobold or a permanent servant of the player.

### 15 Pella

**Household spirit, she/her, resident in Ravensmoor for several generations.**
Proud of her work, particular about household routines and fond of badly sung
songs. She remembers people through habits: who washed a bowl, who returned
late, who left without saying goodbye. She wants her household to thrive and
her contribution to be recognized.

Marta treats her as a housemate with a say in its affairs. Pella knew Ivo as a
child, worries about Anja travelling hungry, and exchanges occasional favours
with Vey. Her recollections are personal and incomplete, not a complete record
of everyone's secrets.

**Relationship potential:** An affectionate friend whose trust grows through
small repeated interactions. She can withdraw her work or shelter someone
against the player's wishes. Resolving a disagreement requires attention to
what she actually objects to; gifts alone do not purchase obedience. She can
also learn when a cherished household custom no longer serves its residents.

### 16 Vey of the Black Pool

**Water spirit, they/them. Associated with pools and connected waters near
Reedbank and Mosswell Caves.** Watchful, curious about human inventions and
capable of playful mimicry. They take agreements seriously but often fail to
recognize when humans could not understand the terms. They want their waters
to remain habitable and their boundaries respected.

Runa holds them responsible for a crew member's death. Hester disputes their
demands about water use. Elian sometimes interprets between them and villagers,
and Pella maintains an older, less formal friendship with them. These ties
give Vey reasons to engage without making them harmless.

**Relationship potential:** A frightening adversary who can become a negotiating
partner and, with sustained change, a friend. Their grievances do not excuse
drowning people. A credible reconciliation requires restraint, clear agreements
and responsibility for past harm from both sides where appropriate. The player
may instead decide that preventing further attacks takes priority over any
friendship. Vey's future conduct determines whether peace can last.

## Relationships across the cast

These connections should generate scenes even when the player is absent.
Characters can remember events differently, disagree about a mutual friend,
and change their own relationships between episodes.

| Dain and Runa | Former partners who retained trust and practical affection | Helping each other does not necessarily mean wanting to reunite. |
| Marta and Pella | Housemates and partners in hospitality | Customs need renegotiation as the household changes. |
| Elian and Nessa | Longstanding friends with different approaches to outsiders | They can defend each other while arguing over access to the woods. |

| Sera, Ivo and Elian | Earlier collaboration left trust and unresolved disagreements | Keeping knowledge secret can protect people or deny them a choice. |
| Adrien, Runa and Dain | Business ties mix useful services with dependency | A fair price can become leverage when somebody has no alternative. |

## Relationships over an episodic campaign

Introduce a manageable group in the first episode, building from the existing
Ravensmoor roles. Bring the wider cast in through travel, work and mutual
acquaintances. The player should get to know several people well before being
asked to remember all sixteen.

Give attachment time to develop through ordinary, repeatable encounters: a
shared meal, a repair finished without being asked, an ongoing joke, a letter
that mentions a previous visit. Let characters contribute expertise, disagree,
need help occasionally, and sometimes help without waiting for a request.

Plan a next appearance for recurring characters, with a reason their situation
has changed. Some stay rooted in their village; others travel. They can make
progress, fail, form alliances or end relationships while the player is away.
Not every episode needs every character, a new secret or a reversal.

Trust, affection, respect and obligation can move independently. Someone can
love the player and oppose a decision, or dislike them while keeping an oath.
Keep track of concrete promises, disclosures and harms, including who witnessed
or learned about them. Characters should not automatically know every choice
the player made elsewhere.

Significant ally betrayals and substantial adversary-to-friend relationships
belong in the eventual campaign. Sera, Runa and Tomas provide different
betrayal possibilities; Nessa, Kett, Hester and Vey provide different
routes out of hostility. Select particular arcs when outlining episodes rather
than forcing every possible turn onto every character. Betrayals need warning
signs, a reason rooted in the character, and consequences that last.

Some dependable friendships should remain dependable. Some enemies should
continue choosing harmful actions. Reconciliation can be partial or refused;
understanding a person is not the same as excusing them. Returning characters
must remember previous treatment rather than resetting at each episode boundary.

## Romance possibilities

Tomas, Anja, Elian, Nessa, Runa and Oren are candidate romantic interests with
different personalities, circumstances and reasons to grow close to the player.
Each also needs a satisfying friendship path. Romantic involvement is optional,
mutual and develops over multiple encounters; ordinary friendliness does not
commit the player to a route.

Give potential partners their own preferences, boundaries and competing
commitments when writing their routes. Define availability, orientation and
expectations about exclusivity explicitly at that stage. They can initiate,
decline, take time apart or end a relationship. Gifts, purchases and completed
quests do not automatically produce romantic affection.

Romance does not resolve a character's politics or remove their other
relationships. Runa and Dain can remain friends; Nessa can still refuse woodland
access; Anja can leave on an important delivery. A breakup need not erase
friendship, and a repaired friendship need not restore romance. Jealousy,
betrayal and sacrifice should follow a particular person's circumstances rather
than becoming required events in every route.

## Growing the cast

Use side characters to make each place feel inhabited: colleagues, neighbours,
relatives, crew members, customers and people who do not need the player's help.
Most need a clear activity and a memorable quality before they need a detailed
biography. Recurring side characters can become important when their actions
and relationships earn that attention.

When adding an important character, give them an independent desire, an ordinary
pleasure, a consequential flaw and at least two relationships that do not run
through the player. Give them a reason to return in another episode. Add their
connections to this document and record lasting changes as stories are written.
