# Game Concept — A Retro Fantasy RPG Engine & Campaign Creator

The project is a **first-person fantasy RPG engine, a campaign creation toolkit, and the first episode of an expandable adventure saga**, inspired by classic games such as *The Elder Scrolls: Arena*, *Eye of the Beholder*, and *Might and Magic*.

The goal is to recreate the atmosphere, exploration, and depth of classic 80s and 90s fantasy RPGs while making it exceptionally easy to create new adventures, locations, characters, and entire campaigns.

## Gameplay

Players explore an interconnected fantasy world consisting of towns, villages, forests, dungeons, castles, and other locations. Each location is built using a simple tile-based map.

Movement is **grid-based with smooth first-person 3D transitions**, combining the structured exploration of classic dungeon crawlers with the immersive presentation of early Elder Scrolls games.

Players create and develop characters with D&D-inspired attributes, skills, equipment, and progression systems. Gameplay revolves around exploration, conversations, quests, trading, combat, puzzles, traps, and discovering secrets.

The world contains interactive NPCs, merchants, companions, enemies, monsters, hidden treasures, and branching storylines. Player decisions can influence quests, relationships, and the state of the world.

## Visual Style

The game uses a distinctive **2.5D retro fantasy aesthetic**, combining simple 3D environments with richly illustrated 2D sprites, textures, and atmospheric effects.

The visual direction evokes classic 80s fantasy adventure artwork and 90s computer RPGs, featuring mature, heroic character designs rather than exaggerated cartoon proportions.

Environments are atmospheric and evocative: medieval villages illuminated by lanterns, wet cobblestone streets reflecting moonlight, ancient stairways, mysterious forests, dark dungeons, and cursed castles silhouetted against dramatic skies.

The rendering system combines:

- Simple modular 3D geometry for buildings, terrain, walls, stairs, and structures.
- Painted and pixel-art textures for environmental detail.
- 2D billboard sprites for NPCs, enemies, monsters, foliage, and decorative objects.
- Dynamic lighting, fog, glowing windows, reflections, and atmospheric effects.
- Painted backgrounds and distant scenery to create the illusion of expansive worlds.

The objective is to achieve a beautiful, cohesive visual style without requiring complex 3D modeling or extensive handcrafted assets.

## World and Campaign System

The world is organized as a **network of interconnected locations**. Each location has its own tile map, visual theme, NPCs, enemies, objects, events, and exits connecting it to other locations.

Campaigns are data-driven and independent of the underlying engine.

The engine provides reusable systems for:

- Character creation, attributes, skills, inventory, and progression.
- Exploration, interaction, and combat.
- NPC dialogue, relationships, and trading.
- Quests, conditions, triggers, and branching narratives.
- Monsters, encounters, traps, puzzles, and loot.
- World state, persistence, and scripted events.

## Campaign Creation Toolkit

A central goal is to make creating RPG adventures accessible without requiring programming or professional art skills.

The toolkit will allow creators to visually design tile-based locations, connect them into worlds, populate them with characters and monsters, define dialogue and quests, configure encounters, and build interactive stories.

Reusable asset libraries, modular environments, visual scripting, and AI-assisted asset creation will help creators produce complete campaigns with minimal effort.

The long-term vision is a platform where creators can build and share their own fantasy adventures using a common RPG engine.

## First Episode

The initial release will include a complete, self-contained fantasy adventure that introduces the world, establishes the saga's mythology, and demonstrates the engine's capabilities.

It will feature interconnected settlements, wilderness areas, and dungeons; memorable characters; quests; exploration; combat; and an overarching mystery.

This first episode will serve both as a standalone game and as a reference campaign demonstrating what can be built with the toolkit.

## Core Philosophy

**Classic RPG depth, modern accessibility, and lightweight content creation.**

The project prioritizes atmosphere, exploration, storytelling, meaningful interactions, and creative freedom over graphical realism or technical complexity.

The ultimate goal is to create not just one RPG, but a reusable foundation for an entire saga—and a toolset that makes building new fantasy adventures almost as enjoyable as playing them.
