Attached: style-game.png (a screenshot of the game) and style-map.png (the game's world map, 320x180 pixel art). Match their pixel-art look: crisp chunky pixels, a limited palette, dark outlines.

Requirements:
- Canvas 1536x1024. The picture fills ONLY the 5:3 band from y=51 to y=972 (full width); paint the strips above and below flat solid magenta (#ff00ff).

- Scene: the old town of Ravensmoor at dusk, seen from a moor outside it. A dark walled town of steep roofs and a church tower on a low hill in the middle distance, a few warm lit windows; a ruined crypt gate half-sunk in the heath in the foreground left; a dead tree on the right with ravens perched; more ravens circling against a cold violet-blue sky with a pale moon and thin clouds; mist lying low over the moor.
- The middle of the picture (where the game writes its title and three menu buttons, roughly the centre 60% of the width and from 25% to 85% of the height) must stay calm and fairly dark: sky, mist and distant silhouettes only, no bright details there. Put the interesting details towards the edges and the bottom.
- The outer 10% on the left and right, and 12% at the top and bottom, may be cropped on some screens: nothing important there.
- NO text, letters, logo, UI, borders or frames.
